Our Essence

Roots. Craft.
An open table.

Behind ART MEDITERRANI is Roberto Lorente Brigido: Mediterranean roots, a culinary career spanning Spain and France, and the pleasure of sharing good food.

Roberto Lorente Brigido

Between two countries.
Rooted in the Mediterranean.

Roberto has built his professional cooking career between Spain and France. That experience meets his roots in the lands of Valencia and Alicante, his home region, where rice is part of the culture and of the way people gather around a table.

His speciality is the rice dishes of Valencia and Alicante. He cooks with respect for ingredients and recipes, paying close attention to the sofrito, the stock, the texture of the grain and the heat. It is a thoughtful, welcoming approach that gives each dish the time it needs.

Through ART MEDITERRANI, he wants to share that enthusiasm: cooking for others and passing on the recipes, skills and techniques that give each rice dish its character. At a celebration or a workshop, the aim is the same: to enjoy cooking, learn together and share the meal.
