04 · An experience to share

Paella & Fideuà Workshop

Learn, cook and share a true Mediterranean moment.

Explore the world of Valencian rice dishes and discover the secrets of authentic paella and flavourful fideuà alongside Roberto.

  • €70 / person
  • Minimum 8 participants
  • Around 3 hours
  • 2 dishes: a rice dish and a fideuà
  • Tasting & shared meal included

Everything is included in your workshop

  • A complete theory booklet to keep: all the recipes prepared, chef’s tips and tricks, rice varieties, rice-to-stock ratios, cooking techniques, and preparation of stocks and broths.
  • Two specialities cooked together: a traditional rice dish and a fideuà, prepared step by step.
  • Unlimited sangria: with and without alcohol, during the workshop.
  • On-site tasting: of both dishes.
  • A relaxed, family-style meal: around the table, sharing the dishes prepared together.

The programme

From selecting ingredients to controlling the heat, including the sofrito, stock preparation and cooking ratios, discover the essential techniques for making rice dishes and fideuà at home.

Much more than a cooking class: a time to learn, share and enjoy Mediterranean flavours.
