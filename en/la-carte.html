Paella & fideuà workshops

Learn, cook and share: discover authentic Mediterranean paella and fideuà. Together we prepare a dish, explore ingredients and techniques, then enjoy the meal.

From €70 / personGroups of 5 to 10 · 2–3 hour workshop + sangria

What you'll learn

  • Choosing rice, ingredients and stock
  • Sofrito, cooking and heat control
  • Dry rice, creamy rice and fideuà techniques
  • Hands-on cooking and tasting

Tailored organisation

2–3 hours of workshop time, plus preparation and set-up beforehand. The cooking area must be suitable and approved from photos or videos.

Birthdays · Friends · Team building · Private celebrations
